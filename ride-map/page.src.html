      Classic routes, leg by leg

      How to mark your ride. Click any road on the model to mark it ridden, or click again to unmark. Turn on Paint roads to drag along a road instead. Tick legs in the list, or import a GPX or KML file from your phone, Strava or a GPS unit; roads your track follows are marked for you. Roads come in pieces of up to 8 km so you can mark just the stretch you rode.

      Sources. Terrain: Terrain Tiles (SRTM and others) via AWS Open Data, about 650 m grid, relief exaggerated. Himachal roads © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Classic route lines: scroll-route-demo by Aditya Sharma (MIT). Lakes: Natural Earth, plus Tso Moriri and Tso Kar traced from the terrain. Rivers are traced from the terrain. Pass heights are the commonly signed figures; heights under the pointer come from the terrain model and read low on sharp peaks. No boundaries are drawn.
